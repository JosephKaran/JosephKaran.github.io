01 // EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ENGINEERING OBJECTIVE

This project focuses on the design, fabrication, and firmware architecture of a scale mechatronic industrial forklift. The goal was to engineer a robust mobile robot capable of both high-torque differential drive locomotion and linear mast elevation, all running on an embedded ESP32 microcontroller with customized power management and wireless telemetry.

02 // HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE & BILL OF MATERIALS
MICROCONTROLLER ESP32 Dev Module (Dual-Core Tensilica Xtensa 32-bit LX6 @ 240 MHz)
MOTOR DRIVER Texas Instruments DRV8833 Dual MOSFET H-Bridge (1.5A RMS per channel)
DRIVE ACTUATION 2x N20 6V Micro Metal Geared Motors (100:1 gear ratio, steel gears)
MAST ELEVATION TowerPro MG90S Micro Servo (1.8 kg-cm torque, metal gear train)
POWER SUPPLY 2S LiPo 7.4V 450mAh Pack + Step-Down Buck Converter (5V output)
CHASSIS & FORKS Additive PLA+ (Bambu Lab A1 Mini, 0.16mm layer height, 4 wall perimeters)

04 // FABRICATION & TESTING FINDINGS

• Mechanical Tolerancing: Printed on a Bambu Lab A1 Mini with a textured PEI spring steel sheet. Mast rack-and-pinion clearances were calibrated to 0.15mm backlash to ensure smooth vertical travel without binding under 200g payload loads.
• Inductive Decoupling: 100uF electrolytic and 0.1uF ceramic capacitors were soldered across the motor terminals and power rails to prevent voltage drops and flyback spikes from causing ESP32 brownouts.
• Firmware Stability: Hardware PWM timers were configured at 2 kHz frequency, preventing audible motor coil whining while providing smooth low-speed positioning.
